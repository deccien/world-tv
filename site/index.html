OTTplayer on iPhone

  1. Install OttPlayertv from the App Store, open it and sign in (create the account at ottplayer.tv if you have none). Add this iPhone as a device when the app asks.
  2. In Safari, sign in at ottplayer.tv → your profile → Playlists → Add playlist.
  3. Give it a name, choose Link / URL as the source, and paste one of the URLs above (start with Lite or Favorites on a phone).
  4. Under Devices, tick your iPhone and save. (Later: the gear icon next to the playlist → Devices.)
  5. Back in the app, pull to refresh / open the playlist list and pick it. Keep auto-update on so the daily rebuild reaches the app.

Some streams are geo-blocked or not 24/7; when one fails, try the next “Name | 2”. Details: build report · report.json
